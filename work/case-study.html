01 / The Challenge

Rising CPMs and creative ad exhaustion

Velocity Apparel was spending heavily on traditional Facebook and Instagram image ads for their Winter outerwear collection. Over three months, their customer acquisition cost doubled as consumers developed severe ad blindness to slick studio lookbooks.

Previous influencer attempts had yielded disappointing results: macro-influencers posted single static Instagram photos that collected thousands of likes from international teenage followers, but generated less than 15 total orders.

02 / The Approach

Deploying The Weave System

Phase 1: Deep Vetting

We audited 75 prospective creators and disqualified 57 for low local follower ratios and suspicious comment engagement. The remaining 18 creators had verified 85%+ resident Bangladeshi audiences with strong purchasing power in Dhaka, Chittagong, and Sylhet.

Phase 2: Hook Testing & Direct Response Briefs

Instead of generic styling shots, each creator filmed vertical 9:16 video try-on hauls highlighting specific seasonal durability, water-resistance tests, and real-life city commuting wear. Every video incorporated three distinct 3-second opening hook variations.

Phase 3: Creator Whitelisting & Paid Amplification

We secured partnership advertising rights to the creators' handles. The top 5 organically performing videos were immediately scaled with paid Meta Spark ads, directing traffic to a personalized creator landing page with an exclusive 12% promo code.

04 / Growth Trajectory

What we'd do next

Following this benchmark sprint, Velocity Apparel transitioned the top 4 performing creators into annual brand ambassador agreements with guaranteed monthly content retainers.

The raw UGC video footage was reorganized into a modular 40-asset b-roll library, allowing Velocity's internal media team to continuously test fresh iterations throughout their Summer rollout without paying new production fees.
